How we invest

No proprietary products. Backed by a research department.

We do not manufacture products, nor do we receive incentives for recommending one over another. Every decision rests on daily market analysis, enhanced by technology but always defined by an accredited advisor.

Independence

Rigor

Daily monitoring of markets, news and instruments, with our own data and method.

Tailored strategy

The process

From data to decision.

Every recommendation goes through the same disciplined, repeatable method.

01

Daily overview

Each day we process more than twenty sources of news, market data and economic indicators, and build a complete picture of what is happening.

02

A research department with AI

It organizes and analyzes all that information and identifies the most attractive assets for the current point in the cycle. It works around the clock.

03

Investment council

The council debates that evidence from every angle and arrives at a well-founded recommendation: what to do and why.

04

The advisor's decision

The advisor receives the recommendation and its supporting reports, reviews the rationale and decides together with the client.
