Comprehensive wealth solutions

Investment

Growing wealth, in Chile and around the world.

Real Estate

Professional real estate exposure, without managing properties.

  • Real estate funds. Income-producing buildings in Chile and the United States.
  • Alternative assets. With a real estate underlying.

Insurance

Real protection for the family and its wealth.

  • Life insurance. Coverage that genuinely protects your family.
  • International health insurance. Access to centers such as Mayo Clinic through the insurer's arrangements, not budget alone.

Wealth Structuring

Holding companies, inheritance and pensions, in order.

  • Inheritance and succession. Family holding companies, gifts and wills.
  • Holding companies. Investment, real estate and holding structures.
  • Pensions. Private dollar pensions, APV and retirement.

Investment, protection and structure are not considered separately. One single strategy.
